What we do

Cost consultancy services

Commercial support across the whole project lifecycle. Take a single service or appoint us from inception to completion.

Cost planning & estimating

Feasibility estimates and elemental cost plans aligned to the RIBA stages and NRM, so the budget is realistic before design gets going.

Bills of quantities

Accurate, well-structured bills of quantities and pricing documents that give tenderers a level playing field and make tender comparison straightforward.

Procurement & tendering

Advice on procurement route and contract form, tender documents, tender analysis and recommendations for appointment.

Contract administration

Administration of JCT and NEC contracts: interim valuations, payment notices, change control and compensation events.

Post-contract cost management

Monthly cost reports, forecasts of final cost, valuation of variations and agreement of final accounts, with no surprises at the end.

Commercial management

Contractor-side surveying: subcontract procurement, applications for payment, cost-value reconciliation and claims preparation.

Due diligence & monitoring

Independent project monitoring for funders and investors, covering cost, programme and risk, with reports on drawdown requests.

Dispute support

Quantum analysis, delay and disruption costing and support through adjudication and negotiated settlement.

Lifecycle & value engineering

Whole-life cost analysis and value engineering workshops that save money without losing what matters to the scheme.

How we work

A clear view at every stage

Most budget problems start early and surface late. We stay close to the numbers throughout, so decisions are made while they can still be changed cheaply.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start with your brief, your budget and your appetite for risk, and agree what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Robust estimates and cost plans set a realistic budget, with risk and contingency shown openly.

  3. 03

    Procure

    The right procurement route and contract, competitive tenders, and a clear recommendation.

  4. 04

    Control

    Valuations, change control and regular cost reports keep the final cost in sight through to the final account.

About Clearview QS

Independent advice, plainly given

Clearview QS is a cost consultancy built on a simple idea: clients deserve a clear, honest picture of what their project will cost and why. We have no interest in the outcome other than getting the numbers right for you.

We work for developers, private clients, public bodies, funders and contractors. Whatever the size of the project, the same senior people who price and report on it are the people you speak to.

  • Independent

    Impartial advice with no ties to contractors or suppliers.

  • Transparent

    Every figure traceable, every assumption written down.

  • Hands-on

    Senior involvement from first meeting to final account.

  • Responsive

    Quick answers when a decision can’t wait.
